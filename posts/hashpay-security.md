# How HashPay Ensures Top-Tier Security for Payment Streaming

Security is at the core of HashPay’s payment streaming platform. We employ advanced cryptographic techniques and blockchain technology to protect every transaction.

## Security Measures
- **End-to-End Encryption**: All data is encrypted during transmission.
- **Smart Contract Audits**: Regular audits ensure code integrity.
- **Decentralized Ledger**: Transactions are immutable and transparent.

HashPay is committed to safeguarding your funds and data. Visit [hashpay.com/security](https://hashpay.com/security) for details.
